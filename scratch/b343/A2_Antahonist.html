FREE Office Desk - 3D Pixel Art Kit

A downloadable asset pack

Download NowName your own price

Free pack from the 3D pixel tech line: same style, scale and light as the paid kits Micro & Electronics and the Repair Shop Bundle. Everything in the store in one purchase: Greatest Bundle of All. Free tools: Antahonist Tools.

36 office sprites: furniture, desk tech, stationery and a small break room, built to real office sizes. Made from real 3D models and rendered straight to pixel art: crisp sel-out outlines, no anti-aliasing, alpha only 0 or 255, one light from the top left.

THE SET

  • Furniture (10): desk with drawer pedestal, office chair, filing cabinet, bookshelf, whiteboard, cork board, standing desk, waste bin, coat rack, water cooler
  • Desk tech (9): monitor, keyboard + mouse, desk phone, laser printer, desk lamp, paper shredder, projector, conference speakerphone, flatbed scanner
  • Stationery (12): stapler, hole punch, tape dispenser, scissors, pen cup, sticky notes, paper ream, ring binder, clipboard, desk calculator, stamp + ink pad, paper clips
  • Break room (5): coffee machine, mug, potted plant, desk fan, snack vending machine

BUILT FROM REAL DIMENSIONS

A 740 mm desk top (EN 527), a 460 mm chair seat, a 1320 mm four-drawer cabinet, A4 paper (ISO 216), a hole punch with ISO 838 80 mm spacing and an 18.9 l water cooler bottle. The dimension table ships in the zip.

WHAT YOU GET

  • Isometric (exact 2:1) and top-down 3/4 views, 32 / 64 / 128 px
  • 8-frame turntable spin per item: PNG strip + GIF + .aseprite (tag "spin")
  • manifest.json with names, categories and sizes

LICENSE

  • You can: use the assets in unlimited personal and commercial projects: games you sell, free games, client work, apps, videos
  • You can: edit, recolour, crop and combine them however you need
  • You can't: resell, share or give away the asset files themselves (as-is or edited), or put them in another asset pack or template
  • No credit required (always welcome). One download covers your whole team. Full text in LICENSE.txt inside the zip.

FAQ

  • Engines: any engine that loads PNG (Godot, Unity, GameMaker, RPG Maker, Construct)
  • Designs: generic, no logos or brand names
