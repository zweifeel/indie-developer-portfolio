ThirdAngle - Isometric Office Starter Pack (FREE)
===============================================================

A free 6-object taster of the ThirdAngle Isometric Office Pack.
13 PNG files: 6 object files, plus a small room-building set,
3 wall pieces in both facings and 1 floor tile.

Enough to read as an office corner. The full pack has 28 objects.

Format:  PNG, transparent background - objects and floors 64x64, walls 64x128
Style:   Isometric pixel art (2:1 dimetric)
Scale:   Every sprite is bottom-anchored at y=59 on its 64x64 canvas, so you
         can drop them straight onto a 64x32 isometric grid.

Building a room
---------------
- floor_office_carpet_tile.png 64x32 diamond in the BOTTOM half of the 64x64 canvas.
                       Place one per grid cell; they tile seamlessly.
- wall_*.png           Wall pieces on a 64x128 canvas, each exactly TWO cells
                       long, 66px high, base at the 2:1 grid slope, no end-caps
                       so a run is seamless. *_right pieces run along the
                       back-right wall: put pixel (0,82) on a cell's top
                       vertex. *_left pieces run along the back-left wall: put
                       pixel (63,82) on the cell's top vertex. Both runs meet
                       in a corner at the back cell.
- wall_end_*.png       4px end-cap for the open end of a run: put pixel (0,114)
                       (_right) or (63,114) (_left) on the vertex where the run
                       ends.
- wall_office_window_* An office window section, on the same anchors as a wall_plain piece.
- Use the same anchor for a floor tile and the object standing on it.
