## Engineering checks used in the models
- The chair seat (460) sits below the 740 desk top so the knees fit; the standing desk top is at 1100.
- Drawers are as deep as the cabinet minus the back panel; paper items are A4 in plan.
- The hole punch dies are 80 mm apart (ISO 838), so a punched A4 sheet fits the ring binder.
