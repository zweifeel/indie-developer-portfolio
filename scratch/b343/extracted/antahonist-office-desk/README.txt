FREE OFFICE DESK - 3D PIXEL ART KIT
by Antahonist - antahonist.itch.io

36 office sprites: furniture, desk tech, stationery and a small break room, built to real office sizes. Every item is a real 3D model rendered straight to pixel art: crisp sel-out outlines, no anti-aliasing,
alpha only 0 or 255, one light from the top left, surface grain and bevelled edges.

BUILT FROM REAL DIMENSIONS
A 740 mm desk top (EN 527), a 460 mm chair seat, a 1320 mm four-drawer cabinet, A4 paper (ISO 216), a hole punch with ISO 838 80 mm spacing and an 18.9 l water cooler bottle (see reference/DIMENSIONS.md).

WHAT'S IN THE BOX
- isometric/png_32, png_64, png_128 - exact 2:1 isometric view
- topdown/png_32, png_64, png_128 - top-down 3/4 view, same models and colours
- spin/<category>/<item>_spin.png - 8-frame turntable strip, plus .gif and .aseprite (tag "spin", 100 ms per frame)
- manifest.json - id, name, category, sizes and spin info for every item
- reference/DIMENSIONS.md - the dimension table the models were built from
- preview/ - the store images

THE SET
- Furniture (10): desk with drawer pedestal, office chair, filing cabinet, bookshelf, whiteboard, cork board, standing desk, waste bin, coat rack, water cooler
- Desk tech (9): monitor, keyboard + mouse, desk phone, laser printer, desk lamp, paper shredder, projector, conference speakerphone, flatbed scanner
- Stationery (12): stapler, hole punch, tape dispenser, scissors, pen cup, sticky notes, paper ream, ring binder, clipboard, desk calculator, stamp + ink pad, paper clips
- Break room (5): coffee machine, mug, potted plant, desk fan, snack vending machine

NOTES
- All designs are generic: no logos, brand names or copied products.
- Spin frames share one scale and centre, so the model turns in place.
- The images were rendered by our own generator; the generator and processing code were written with AI assistance.
