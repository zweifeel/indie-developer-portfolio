## 3. ASSET PIPELINE & INTEGRATION INSTRUCTIONS

All assets are stored locally in `public/assets/virtual-office/` and served statically by the Vite frontend:
- Pixel-preservation rendering rule: `image-rendering: pixelated; shape-rendering: crispEdges;`
- Projection: Strict 2:1 dimetric ratio ($2 \times \Delta x = \Delta y$).
- Scalability: Vector crispness at all DPIs with razor-sharp pixel edges.
